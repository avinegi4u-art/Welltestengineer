Governing equations

m = 162.6 · qBμ / (k·h)Semilog slope (psi/cycle). The middle-time straight line on the Horner/derivative plot.
k = 162.6 · qBμ / (m·h)Permeability from the slope.
s = 1.151 · [ (p1hr − pwf)/m − log₁₀( k / (φμctrw²) ) + 3.23 ]Skin factor. p₁ₕᵣ is read from the straight line at Δt = 1 hr.
p* = intercept at (tp+Δt)/Δt → 1Extrapolated (false) reservoir pressure — the y-intercept of the Horner line.
Δpskin = 0.87 · m · s · FE = (p* − pwf − Δpskin)/(p* − pwf)Pressure loss from skin and flow efficiency.
C = qB·Δt / (24·Δp) · CD = 0.8936·C / (φcthrw²)Wellbore storage coefficient from the early unit-slope line, and its dimensionless form.
rinv = 0.029 · √( k·Δt / (φμct) )Radius of investigation at the last analyzed time.
Bourdet: Δp′ = d(Δp)/d(ln te), te = tpΔt/(tp+Δt)Pressure derivative on equivalent time; the radial-flow plateau equals m/2.303.

How to read the diagnostics

Wellbore storage (early time)

Both Δp and the derivative rise together on a 1:1 unit slope. Data in this window cannot see the formation yet — exclude it from the radial-flow fit. The storage coefficient C and CD are estimated from this line.

Infinite-acting radial flow (middle time)

The derivative flattens to a horizontal plateau at m/2.303. This is the window that gives k, skin, and p*. Box-select these points on the Horner or log-log chart to lock the fit.

Boundaries / depletion (late time)

A derivative that turns upward (slope → 1) signals a no-flow boundary or depletion; a downward dip can indicate constant-pressure support or dual porosity. Keep these points out of the radial fit but use them for boundary/limit checks.

Skin, PI and flow efficiency

Positive skin = damage (Δpskin > 0, FE < 1); negative skin = stimulation. PI is the productivity index q/(p*−pwf). Large |skin| usually means completion damage, partial penetration, or unresolved multiphase cleanup.

Radius of investigation

rinv ≈ 0.029·√(kΔt/φμct) estimates how deep the transient has probed at the last analyzed time — a rough sense of the volume the test represents.
