Volume equations

DP volume = DP length × DP capacity
HWDP volume = HWDP length × HWDP capacity
DC volume = DC length × DC capacity
D = DP + HWDP + DC
F = (DC × OH) + (DP/HWDP × OH)
G = DP × casing
H = F + G; I = D + H; Total active = I + J
Strokes = Volume / Pump displacement; Time = Strokes / Slow pump rate
