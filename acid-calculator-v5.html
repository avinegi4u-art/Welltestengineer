    Field QC & Safety Reminders

    Summarised from standard acidizing QC / safety guidance. Tick off in pre-job review and pre-pump meeting.

    Before pumping
    • Equipment rig-up, pressure test, and bleed paths verified.
    • Final acid recipe (HCl %, additives) checked vs program.
    • Corrosion test at temperature completed for tubular metallurgy.
    • Compatibility tests done (brine–acid–crude / Fe / scale).
    • Iron / solids in lines flushed or displaced before main acid.
    During / after pumping
    • Density / rate trending monitored vs design, adjust spend / diversion.
    • Pressure limits for casing / packer respected, no screenout-style spike.
    • Spent acid flowback sampled, clarity / Fe / oil cut noted.
    • Post-job PI / drawdown and skin estimate planned (build-up / test).
