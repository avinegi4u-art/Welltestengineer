Buoyed weight & cumulative WIR

Hook load in well fluid while RIH — metal weight with buoyancy, not air weight. Cumulative sum from surface downward as each item enters the brine.

Dry (lb) = 2.670 × (OD² − ID²) × Lft  |  BF = 1 − (fluid ppg ÷ steel ppg)  |  Buoyed = Dry × BF × submerged fraction  |  WIRcum = Σ buoyed
